# Carousel Time

A Garmin Connect IQ watch face that shows your chosen metrics on a turning carousel.

- Up to eight metrics sit as small values around the bezel.
- The one under the START button is shown magnified in the centre, with more detail: a sparkline for heart rate, stress or Body Battery, progress to goal for steps or floors.
- Every few seconds the carousel turns and a new metric comes up.

The carousel behaves as if it ran continuously. When you raise your wrist, it carries on from the metric you were already looking at and turns every few seconds (configurable). In always-on mode it moves one step per minute.
